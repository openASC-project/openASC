Use your own Assetto Corsa installation

openASC includes no cars, tracks, textures, or sounds. The content tools read files from your local copy of Assetto Corsa (2014).

A project at the beginning

The inventory tool, documentation, and website are available. Rendering, vehicle physics, and driving are still ahead. Finding a file does not establish compatibility.

What is openASC?

openASC is an open-source effort to build an independent engine for Assetto Corsa content. Inspired by the approach of openOMSI, development starts with understanding file formats and validating one small scene before moving toward a first drive.

The current tool inventories local car and track directories without changing the installation. There is no playable simulator yet, and full mod compatibility is a long-term research goal.
